Privacy Policy

1. Overview

ChatSaver.ai is built to protect your privacy. The Free plan is 100% local: your data stays on your device. The Pro plan adds cloud sync that is end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read your conversations. This policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Information We Collect

ChatSaver.ai stores your data on your device using your browser's localStorage. This includes:

  • Conversation titles and content
  • User-defined profiles and preferences
  • Application settings

If you email us or join the launch list, we also receive your email address and the message you send, which we use only to reply or to tell you when the mobile apps launch.

3. Data Storage and Transmission

Free: No conversation data is transmitted to any external servers. Everything stays on your personal device, and the developer has no access to it.

Pro: If you turn on cloud sync, your conversations are encrypted on your device before they leave it. We store only encrypted data that we cannot read.

4. Third-Party Services

ChatSaver.ai does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services, and we don't sell your data. The AI platforms you save chats from (such as ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Kimi and Perplexity) have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

5. Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You can export your chats, delete any conversation, or clear all stored data at any time, either in the app or by clearing the application's localStorage in your browser settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the bottom of this page.
