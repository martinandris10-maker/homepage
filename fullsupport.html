Quick Answers

How do I save a chat?+

Open any AI app, tap the save button, and the entire conversation is saved to your library in one tap.

I hit my 25 chat limit. What now?+

Free saves up to 25 conversations. You can export your chats as a backup, delete ones you no longer need, or go Pro for unlimited conversations.

How do I move my chats to a new phone?+

With Pro, your library syncs to the cloud so you can pick up on any device. Free users can export their chats as a backup first.

How do I cancel Pro?+

Pro is billed monthly and can be canceled anytime from your account settings. No contracts, no hidden fees.

Can I lock individual chats?+

Yes. You can set a PIN on any sensitive chat, separate from your overall app password.
