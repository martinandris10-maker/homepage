About ChatSaver.AI

Our Mission

ChatSaver.AI is designed to give users complete ownership and privacy over their conversations with artificial intelligence. By operating entirely locally on your device, we ensure your data remains private and under your control.

Key Features

• Local-First Architecture:
All conversations and profiles are stored exclusively on your device.
• Context Profiles:
Create reusable profiles that help AI models understand your needs and communication style more effectively.
• Multi-AI Support:
Save conversations across different AI platforms in one secure location.
• Complete Data Control:
You maintain full ownership of your data with the ability to export or delete it at any time.

How it Works

ChatSaver.AI acts as a personal memory layer between you and various AI systems. Instead of losing context between conversations, you can maintain rich, persistent profiles and notes that travel with each new interaction.
