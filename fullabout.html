About ChatSaver.ai

Your AI chats. Your library. Your rules.

Our Mission

ChatSaver.ai was built for heavy AI users who want to own their conversations. We give you complete ownership and privacy over everything you say to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Kimi, Perplexity and more, so your best ideas stay yours.

On the Free plan, ChatSaver.ai runs entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked.

Key Features

Local-First Architecture

On Free, all conversations and profiles are stored exclusively on your device, with no cloud and no tracking.

Master Memory Profile

Build one reusable profile from all your chats across every AI, so new AI tools can get to know you faster.

Multi-AI Support

Save conversations from different AI platforms in one searchable library.

Complete Data Control

You keep full ownership of your data, with the ability to export or delete it at any time.

Private by Design, in Both Plans

Free is 100% private and 100% local. It never touches the cloud and stays on your device, forever.

Pro adds end-to-end encrypted cloud sync so you can move between devices. Your chats are encrypted on your device before they leave it, so even we can't read them.

How it Works

ChatSaver.ai acts as a personal memory layer between you and your AI tools. Instead of losing context between conversations, you keep a rich, persistent library and profile that travels with you to every new interaction.
