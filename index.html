FAQ

Which AI apps does ChatSaver support? +

ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Gemini, Kimi, Perplexity, and any other AI chat app you use. If it has a conversation, we can save it.

Is my data really private? +

Yes. On Free, everything is saved locally on your device and never touches the cloud. On Pro, cloud sync is end-to-end encrypted, so your conversations stay unreadable to anyone but you.

Can I cancel Pro anytime? +

Absolutely. Pro is billed monthly and can be canceled anytime from your account settings. No contracts, no hidden fees.

What happens to my saved chats if I switch phones? +

With Pro, your library syncs to the cloud so you can pick up right where you left off on any device. Free users can export their chats as a backup.

Can I lock individual chats? +

Yes. You can set a PIN on any sensitive chat to keep it protected, separate from your overall app password.

Does ChatSaver work offline? +

Yes. Since chats are stored locally by default, you can view and search your saved conversations even without an internet connection.

What happens if I downgrade from Pro? +

Your chats stay safe. You'll keep access to your most recent 25 conversations locally, and anything beyond that remains in your export or cloud backup.
